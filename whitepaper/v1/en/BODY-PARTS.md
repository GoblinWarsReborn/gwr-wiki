# Body Parts

Each goblin has **5 body parts** and these parts influence attributes depending on the race.\
You have the possibility to make **7,776 unique variations** of Goblins, apart from the changes in colors and equipment. 🎨🧬

The **Goblin Wars skill tree** will follow the **3 roles** in a horde a Goblin can take, with several specialized paths.\
All skills will be based on the basic attributes:

* 💪 Strength
* 🌀 Agility
* 🛡️ Stamina
* 🧠 Intelligence
* 👁️ Perception
* 🗣️ Charisma

***

#### 🧍 Parts of a Goblin's Body that can Change:

***

#### 🧬 Races

* 🌲 **Forest** (Int+1, Per+1, Str-1, Agi-1)
* 🪓 **Savage** (Agi+1, Int+1, Vig-1, Per-1)
* 🏜️ **Desert** (Str+1, Vig+1, Cha-1, Per-1)
* 🗻 **Mountain** (Vig+1, Per+1, Cha-1, Int-1)
* 🌑 **Dark** (Agi+1, Int+1, Str-1, Vig-1)
* 🌊 **Sea** (Cha+1, Per+1, Str-1, Vig-1)

***
