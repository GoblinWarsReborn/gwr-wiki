### Desert Goblins

Desert goblins are albino, dark, life-force, and animal-looking creatures. 🌑🐾 As they live isolated, they are disturbed, their senses highly developed. 👁️🧠

Like the Forest Goblin they are highly linked to magic, but less agile and noticeable. 🔮⚡👤
